# Project: Develop a new website for Proense Seguros, a Colombian insurance brokerage with 25+ years of experience.

## Primary Goals:
- Lead Generation: Attract new potential clients (especially families and young professionals interested in Life, Health, and Car insurance) and enable them to easily contact the broker or schedule a consultation.
- Content Marketing & SEO: Establish Proense Seguros as a knowledgeable authority in the Colombian insurance market, attracting organic search traffic and educating potential/existing clients through valuable blog content. Blog content will be managed via a dedicated backend system (WordPress) and displayed seamlessly within the main website experience built with Lovable/Windsurf.
- Existing Client Services: Provide a secure way for existing clients to submit information for policy issuance and request policy modifications, rehabilitations, cancellations, or claims information via online forms (potentially leveraging third-party services) with e-signature capabilities where required.
- Broker Value Proposition: Emphasize personalized assessment, claims support, policy updates, and renewal reminders throughout the site.

## Target Audience

- New Clients: Primarily families and young professionals in Colombia seeking Life, Health, and Car insurance. Also includes general individuals and businesses interested in the broader product range. Attracted via direct traffic, referrals, ad campaigns, and organic search (supported by blog content).
- Existing Clients: A diverse base including families, businesses, and young professionals requiring policy servicing. May also engage with blog content for information and updates.
