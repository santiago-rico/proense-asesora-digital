# Task: Content Audit and Optimization for Proense Seguros Insurance Website

## Background Context
Proense Seguros is a Colombian insurance brokerage with 25+ years of experience. The website aims to:
1. Generate leads from families and young professionals (Life, Health, Car insurance focus)
2. Establish SEO authority through content marketing
3. Provide secure client services for policy management
4. Emphasize broker value: personalized assessment, claims support, renewal reminders

## Your Assignment

### Step 1: Navigation and Content Extraction
Use Playwright MCP to navigate to ALL insurance type pages on the website and extract the complete text content from each page, including:
- Main headings and subheadings
- Coverage descriptions
- Benefits listed
- "¿Para Quién es Este Seguro?" sections
- FAQ content

### Step 2: Coverage Accuracy Verification
For each insurance type page:
1. List all mentioned coverages and benefits
2. Cross-reference with Colombian market standards by researching:
   - SURA Colombia insurance products
   - Allianz Colombia insurance products
   - Seguros Bolívar insurance products
3. Flag any coverages that:
   - Don't exist in the Colombian market
   - Are incorrectly described
   - Use non-standard terminology

### Step 3: Persona Section Analysis
Evaluate each "¿Para Quién es Este Seguro?" section:
1. Count current number of personas per insurance type
2. Identify overlapping or overly specific personas
3. Propose consolidated personas that are:
   - More inclusive and general
   - Still specific enough to resonate
   - Limited to 3-4 per insurance type maximum

### Step 4: Goal Alignment Assessment
Verify content supports website objectives:
- Lead generation: Clear CTAs, consultation scheduling options
- SEO: Keyword optimization, educational value
- Trust building: Professional tone, expertise demonstration
- Value proposition: Broker benefits clearly stated

### Step 5: FAQ Optimization
Analyze current FAQ sections:
1. Research actual search queries for Colombian insurance (use Google Keyword Planner data if available)
2. Identify high-value SEO questions missing from current FAQs
3. Remove or consolidate redundant questions
